AI usage comes in many forms, but not all are correct or beneficial to the user. AI is best used to analyze large portions of raw data to identify specific trends and process it into digestible information for the end user, allowing them to make an informed decision in their best interest. AI can speed up the process by removing the middle-man in the data-to-information pipeline.

Response from an AI:

The core idea is sound: AI is genuinely good at compressing large datasets into something a person can act on, and that’s one of its strongest use cases. But the reasoning has a few weak points. First, “not all are correct or beneficial” is asserted but never defined. Without criteria for what makes a use correct, the claim that analysis and summarization is the best use is hard to test. Best by what measure: accuracy, time saved, value created? Second, “removing the middle-man” glosses over what the middle-man was doing. Analysts don’t just process data; they catch bad inputs, question assumptions, and know the context behind the numbers. If AI replaces that step, the errors and biases don’t vanish, they just become harder to see. A summary that looks clean can hide a flawed trend or a misread dataset, and the end user often can’t tell. Third, the argument undersells other valuable uses, like drafting, tutoring, coding assistance, and brainstorming, where AI works alongside a person rather than as a data pipeline. A stronger version of your claim would be that AI works best as an accelerator of human judgment, with outputs the user can verify, rather than as a replacement for the intermediary steps that keep the decision trustworthy.

Incorrect AI Usage

Replacing sensitive positions
Some stations require human instinct and intuition, something an artificial intelligence cannot replicate. An AI does not know morality or emotion; it only follows orders without its own code of ethics. Letting an AI make decisions in these fields could bring harmful consequences.

Unsupervised use
AI is meant to help humans, not replace them. Humans must closely follow after the AI to ensure it continues to provide meaningful information. Unsupervised use of AI can lead to small errors propagating and worsening over several iterations.

AI replacing human knowledge
Humanity must learn alongside the AI. It cannot replace human curiosity for knowledge. Humans must understand the discoveries made by AI and learn to apply them on their own. If someone does not understand what an AI is doing, they have learned nothing from it and cannot build upon its lessons.

AI is a great tool that can assist people in many ways. it can remove the tedious and monotonous processes while allowing humans to focus on the bigger picture. However, it must be used cautiously; it does not understand emotion, morality, or boundaries. Humans must clearly set limits on what AI can or cannot access to ensure it remains productive and does not become destructive of their progress.
