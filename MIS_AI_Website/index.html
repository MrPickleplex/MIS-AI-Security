This site provides an overview of the use of artificial intelligence (AI) in management information systems (MIS). MIS refers to the many ways information can be used to increase the effectiveness of business decision-making. It is a tool that integrates machine, man, and knowledge to improve an organization’s competitive advantage. AI provides an edge in MIS by quickly analyzing data through its pattern-seeking abilities and identifying trends, assisting human strategists, and automate menial processes that allow humans to focus on more important tasks.

AI-assisted approach: Welcome to our overview of artificial intelligence (AI) in management information systems (MIS). MIS encompasses the many ways information can be used to strengthen business decision-making, integrating technology, people, and knowledge to build an organization’s competitive advantage. AI elevates these systems by rapidly analyzing data, uncovering patterns and trends, and supporting human strategists with deeper insights. By automating routine tasks, it also frees teams to focus on higher-value work that drives meaningful results.
